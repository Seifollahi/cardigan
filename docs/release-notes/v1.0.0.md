# Cardigan 1.0.0 — the wallet you wear

Your loyalty cards, on your wrist, as barcodes that actually scan.

Leave the keyring fobs at home. Cardigan keeps your cards on your Pebble as
real **Code 128 barcodes and QR codes**, rendered pixel-perfect for e-paper
and readable by supermarket lasers and phone cameras alike.

## One press at the till

Cardigan opens straight to your **last-used card** — from the launcher to a
scannable code in a single press. Up/Down hops between your favourites while
you're still in the queue. No phone in your hand, no signal needed, works
entirely offline.

Everything else is one press deeper: Select flips to the balance page, Back
shows the full list, and holding Select opens favourite / set-quick-card /
delete.

## Manage cards from your phone

Open the Pebble app → Cardigan → settings. Add, edit, reorder and colour-code
your cards, choose barcode or QR, and hit save — they land on the watch in
about a second. The editor refuses codes the watch couldn't render rather
than letting you find out at the checkout.

Up to 12 cards live on the watch.

## Every Pebble, square or round

All seven platforms: the 2013 original and Steel (aplite), Time and Time
Steel (basalt), Time Round (chalk), Pebble 2 (diorite), **Pebble Time 2**
(emery), **Pebble 2 Duo** (flint) and **Pebble Round 2** (gabbro).

Long codes rotate automatically to use the full screen height. On the round
watches, codes are fitted to the *circle* rather than a rectangle, so nothing
disappears under the bezel. If a barcode physically cannot fit your display,
Cardigan switches that card to a QR code by itself.

And if nothing fits, it shows the number in large type rather than a cropped
barcode — a truncated Code 128 can carry a valid checksum for a **different,
shorter number**, so a wrong barcode is worse than none.

## Tested by an actual scanner

Every build renders the watchapp's real screen at all four display
geometries, then decodes those frames with zxing — a genuine barcode reader.
If the text doesn't come back byte-for-byte, the build fails. That check
caught the round-display clipping bug above before it ever shipped.

Current status: 20 encoder assertions, 15 app-logic checks, 24 scanner
decodes, all green.

## Privacy

No accounts, no network requests, no analytics, no tracking. Your card
numbers live in the Pebble app's storage on your phone and in the watch's
own persistent storage — nowhere else. See
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Seifollahi/cardigan/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Install

- **Rebble store** — search "Cardigan" under Tools.
- **Sideload** — download `cardigan.pbw` below, share it to the Pebble app on
  your phone, and it will offer to install. With the SDK:
  `pebble install --phone <your-phone-ip>`.

Then open the settings page and add your first card.

## Known limitations

- Balances are entered by hand. There's no universal loyalty-points API, and
  scraping retailer logins isn't something this app is going to do.
- Adding a card means typing its number — there's no camera scanning, because
  the card editor lives inside the Pebble app rather than a standalone
  companion app.
- Code 128 and QR only. No Code 39, EAN, PDF417 or Aztec yet.
- 12 cards maximum, set by the watch's persistent-storage budget.
